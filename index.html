Privacy Policy: Pinterest x Figma

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Overview

At Suba Software Solutions, we respect your privacy. This plugin allows you to search and import images from Pinterest directly into Figma. We do not store your personal Pinterest credentials or track your private design files.

Data Usage

The plugin interacts with the Pinterest API solely to retrieve public images based on your search queries as shown in image_e3a85d.jpg.

  • No Account Tracking: We do not log your searches or associate them with your identity.
  • Figma Data: All image imports occur locally within your Figma file; Suba Software Solutions has no access to your project content.
  • Third-Party APIs: Use of this plugin is subject to Pinterest’s own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please reach out to the team at Suba Software Solutions.
